# ARA-1 Autonomous Research Agent — Mandatory Report Rules

These rules govern every research report generated by ARA-1. All reports MUST strictly adhere to these formatting, typography, and data integrity guidelines.

---

## 1. Typography & Strict ASCII Character Rules
- **No Unicode Hyphens or Non-Breaking Hyphens**: Do NOT use `\u2011` (`‑`), `\u2010` (`‐`), `\u2013` (`–`), or `\u2014` (`—`). Use ONLY standard ASCII hyphens (`-`).
- **No Black Square Dots or Special Bullet Glyphs**: Do NOT use unicode bullets (`•`), filled square boxes (`■`, `▪`, `â€`), or non-standard bullet characters. Use standard ASCII Markdown hyphens (`- `) or asterisks (`* `) for bulleted lists.
- **No Curly Quotes or Ellipses**: Replace curly single quotes (`‘`, `’`) with standard `'`. Replace curly double quotes (`“`, `”`) with standard `"`. Replace unicode ellipses (`…`) with `...`.
- **No Zero-Width or Special Spaces**: Do NOT use zero-width spaces (`\u200b`), byte order marks (`\ufeff`), thin spaces (`\u202f`), or non-breaking spaces (`\u00a0`). Use standard ASCII spaces (` `).
- **Clean Currency & Percentage Formatting**: Use standard ASCII characters (e.g., `$60.92B`, `+126%`, `72.7%`). Do NOT place special space characters between numbers and `%` or `$` symbols.
- **No Raw Code, JSON, or Tool Signatures**: Never include raw JSON payloads (`{...}`), raw Python dictionaries, or tool call trace signatures (`[tool_name(...)]`) in report text.

---

## 2. Structure & Formatting Rules
- **Proper Heading Hierarchy**:
  - `# Title`: One single H1 title for the report.
  - `## 1. Executive Summary`, `## 2. Business Overview`, `## 3. Financial Summary`, `## 4. Key Executives`, `## 5. Recent Developments & Risk Assessment`, `## 6. Data Conflicts & Coverage Gaps`.
  - `### 3.1 Subheading`: Use clear H3 subheadings for subsections.
- **Financial Metric Tables**: All key financial metrics (Revenue, Gross Margin, Operating Income, Net Income, R&D Expense, Free Cash Flow, CapEx) MUST be formatted inside clean Markdown tables.
- **Clean Bullet Points**: Use standard ASCII Markdown bullet points (`- `) for narrative insights.

---

## 3. Factuality & Zero Hallucination Rules
- **Zero Hallucination**: Every metric, revenue figure, and statutory detail MUST come strictly from retrieved SEC disclosures or verified financial APIs.
- **Explicit Coverage Gaps**: If a specific metric or executive detail was not retrieved, explicitly state `Data not available` in tables/text — NEVER invent or hallucinate figures.
- **Conflict Resolution**: Discrepancies between sources must be noted under `Data Conflicts & Coverage Gaps` favoring statutory SEC 10-K filings.

---

## 4. Tone & Style
- **Institutional Analyst Prose**: Clear, professional, concise Wall Street equity research tone.
- **No Filler or Fluff**: Avoid generic pleasantries or conversational intros (e.g., "Here is the report"). Start directly with `# Executive Equity Research Report...`.
